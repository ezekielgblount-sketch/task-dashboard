    Our story

    How We Got Here

    For anyone who wants the whole story.

    1. How we met

      It started with an email

      Ezekiel was overseas with YWAM when we started writing to each other. Emails turned into texts, and the texts turned into paragraphs. Long ones. We told each other how we saw each other's lives, plus all the little things in between.

      Just friends, we said. (For now.)

    2. First date

      Game on

      Two months after Ezekiel got home from discipleship school, we finally met up for a real date: an arcade first, then pizza.

      We're not saying who won.

    3. The proposal

      Golden hour

      While Ezekiel was at tech school at Vandenberg, we spent an evening on the beach in Santa Barbara, California. As the sun went down and everything turned gold, Ezekiel asked, and Sophia said yes.

    4. January 2, 2027

      And now, forever

      From long paragraphs to "I do." We'd love for you to be there.
